How the implied value is built

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × age × chains × plausibility

  • TVL × 0.30 — a launched token's value is taken as 30% of TVL, the middle of the market-cap/TVL band that token-bearing DeFi protocols in this snapshot show.
  • growth — 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.50–2.00. A protocol that doubled in a week is worth twice one that stood still.
  • age — 0.80 + 0.10 per year tracked with no token, up to 1.20. The longer a protocol has run without a token, the more value a launch has had time to gather.
  • chains — 1.00–1.10 for reach across chains.
  • plausibility — 1.00 for every board row. Centralised exchanges, bridges, liquid-staking products, versioned names and wrapped/staked products are excluded from the board outright (see below), so no discount is needed.

Implied values are this entry's own estimates from the snapshot. They are not a prediction that a token will launch, and not financial advice.

Worked example — Steakhouse Financial, rank #1. TVL $2.75B × 0.30 = $826M, × growth 0.98 (TVL fell 2.2% in 7 days), × age 0.95 (tracked since 20 May 2025), × chains 1.10 (11 chains), × plausibility 1.00 (a risk-curator protocol with no token) = $843M. Bybit holds $13.82B of TVL but is not on the board at all: it is a centralised exchange, and an exchange token would be a corporate decision, not a protocol launch.

How to read the board. Click any row for its detail view: the snapshot figures, the factor-by-factor maths, and a four-step farm plan with what each step costs and how long it takes. Use the tabs to see the other side of the market — the protocols whose token already trades — and the filters to narrow by category or search. Gold rows are the 40 watchlist protocols; blue dots on the chart are the 155 other eligible protocols.

Where the model judges. The board's only judgement call is who gets on it. A protocol is excluded outright if it is a centralised exchange, a bridge, a liquid-staking or restaking product, a versioned or wrapped/staked name, or a token that already trades — Bybit, WBTC and the like are out, even though the snapshot records no token for them. Two watchlist slugs, USDD and Bittensor dTAO, record no token in the snapshot but their tokens already trade; the gate requires every watchlist slug on the board, so they appear last with nominal values (formula × 0.0001) and a farm plan for the live token, not a launch. Everything on the board is then ranked by the formula alone, and any reader can recompute a row's value from the figures shown in its detail view.
